RBAC Scope Isolation Rule: Users & Roles are bound strictly to their assigned module and branch permissions. Clinic staff cannot access Lab internal operations, and Lab technicians cannot access Clinic EHR/billing records unless explicitly multi-assigned by the Tenant Owner.

Cross-Module Interoperability Bridge (Unified Clinic + Lab Tenants)

When a tenant subscribes to both Clinic and Lab modules, Clinic Doctors can dispatch Lab Work Orders directly from the patient treatment chart. The Lab manufactures the prosthesis, sends live status callbacks, and attaches the completed restoration back into the patient's clinic record.
